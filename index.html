About

anarchydev.games is a one-man studio run by me, Piotrek Kosmala. I create and publish new games for Atari 8-bit computers, crafted with the same care as modern releases. While I'm a one-man operation, I collaborate with many incredibly talented people from the demoscene, including Piotr Świerszcz (Tatqoo/Taquart/NG/Moonshine), who composed the music for WITTICH.

My first commercial game came out on the Amiga in 1996. Since then I’ve worked on many titles in roles ranging from artist and developer to art director, on platforms from 8-bit computers and the Amiga to PC, Xbox, PlayStation and Nintendo Switch, including the hit SUPERHOT and the acclaimed Liberated.

I’ve also been part of the Atari demoscene since the 1990s, known as Insert. I later spent time in the Amiga and PC scenes, and I’m still active in demoscene productions today.
