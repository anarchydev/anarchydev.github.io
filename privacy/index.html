Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page explains what data anarchydev.games collects when you visit it, why, and how you can change your mind at any time.

Who is responsible

The website anarchydev.games is run by Piotr Kosmala (anarchydev.games), Poland. If you have any questions about your data, write to anarchydevgames@gmail.com.

Statistics (Google Analytics) – only if you agree

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and understand which parts of the site are interesting. It is switched on only after you click “Accept” in the cookie banner. If you click “Reject” or ignore the banner, nothing is sent to Google and no analytics cookies are stored.

When you accept, Google Analytics collects:

  • pages you view and how far you scroll,
  • clicks on links (for example to our Facebook, YouTube or Discord, or the e-mail link),
  • the website that sent you here,
  • approximate location (country, city), device type, browser and language.

Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. We do not use the data for advertising, we do not combine it with other data and we do not sell it. The statistics are kept for 14 months.

Cookies set by Google Analytics: _ga and _ga_<ID>, kept for up to 13 months. Google acts as our data processor; data may be processed in the United States under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. More: Google Privacy Policy.

The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

Changing your choice

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time: open cookie settings (the same link is in the footer of every page). Your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage (analytics-consent), so we don’t have to ask on every visit.

Other services used by the site

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may log technical data such as your IP address for security purposes.
  • Fonts and icons: fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and icons from cdnjs (Cloudflare). Your browser connects to these servers to download them, which reveals your IP address to them. They do not set cookies.
  • External links: links to Facebook, YouTube, Discord, Demozoo and others take you to those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your data, to restrict or object to processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. You can also lodge a complaint with the Polish data protection authority (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych, uodo.gov.pl).
